Sales Leadership Playbook · UX concept · Wireframes

A playbook leaders use in the moment, not one they read once

The content stays the same as in a PDF. What changes is how fast a leader gets from "I have a pipeline review in 10 minutes" to the right agenda, questions and checklist. Below: why clickable, the content map, then wireframes of the navigation options and key screens.

Rules that keep it simple
1Two clicks to any tool. No menus inside menus.
2One topic, one screen. Same page template everywhere, so it's learned once.
3Nothing to install, no extra login. A link on the intranet or in CRM.
4Works on a phone in the car park before a visit.
5Interactive only where it saves time: tabs, checklists, expand/collapse. No gamification, no animations.

3Less LMS, more immersive: three directions
What's causing the LMS feel
The prototype treats every page the same: a unit to finish. Counters, ticks, "Mark as complete", previous/next and the completion message all reward moving on, not thinking. It also puts the big topics (what leadership means) and the working tools (meeting guides) in the same list, so both feel like coursework.
Proposed fix, whichever direction you pick
RemoveProgress %, chapter counts, ticks, "Mark as complete", the completion message
SplitRead (immersive big topics) and Use (meeting guides, cards, glossary: fast and functional, as they are now)
ReplaceProgress with private reflection notes. "Mark as complete" with "Take it to your team"
KeepA quiet "Pick up where you left off" link for returning visitors, with no score attached
